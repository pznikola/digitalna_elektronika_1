\[t_p=Nt_{p0}\left(1+\frac f\gamma\right)
=Nt_{p0}\left(1+\frac{\sqrt[N]F}\gamma\right)\]
\alert{Fiksiramo $N$} i biramo odnose dimenzija za minimalno kašnjenje.
\[f=\sqrt[N]{\frac{C_L}{C_{G,1}}},\qquad
f_1=1,\quad f_2=\lfloor f+0.5\rfloor,\quad f_3=\lfloor f^2+0.5\rfloor,\ldots\]
$\lfloor x\rfloor$ — ceo deo; $\lfloor x+0.5\rfloor$ — najbliži ceo broj.
\[t_p=Nt_{p0}+\frac{t_{p0}}\gamma\sum_{i=1}^N\frac{f_{i+1}}{f_i}\]
