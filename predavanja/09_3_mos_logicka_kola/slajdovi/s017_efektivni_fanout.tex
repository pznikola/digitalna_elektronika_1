\[C_{int1}=\gamma C_{G1},\qquad f=\frac{C_{G2}}{C_{G1}}\quad\text{— efektivni fanout}\]
$\gamma$ zavisi od tehnologije.
\[t_{p1}=t_{p0}\left(1+\frac{C_{ext1}}{\gamma C_{G1}}\right)
=t_{p0}\left(1+\frac f\gamma\right)\]
Skaliranje i-tog invertora za $k$:
\[R_{eq,i}=\frac{R_{eq1}}k,\qquad C_{int,i}=kC_{int1}\]
\[t_{p,i}=t_{p0}\left(1+\frac{C_{G,i+1}}{\gamma C_{G,i}}\right)
=t_{p0}\left(1+\frac{f_{i+1}}\gamma\right)\]
\end{frame}
