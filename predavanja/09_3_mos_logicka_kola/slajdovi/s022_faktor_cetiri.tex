\[f=\sqrt[N]F\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
N=\frac{\ln F}{\ln f}=\frac{\ln(C_L/C_{G,1})}{\ln4}\]

\[f_1=1,\qquad f_2=4,\qquad f_3=16,\ldots\]
