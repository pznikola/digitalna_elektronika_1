\[r=\frac{R_{p1(\beta=1)}}{R_{n1}}=\frac{\frac34\frac{V_{DD}}{I_{Dpsat}}\left(1-\frac79|\lambda_p|V_{DD}\right)}{\frac34\frac{V_{DD}}{I_{Dnsat}}\left(1-\frac79\lambda_nV_{DD}\right)}\approx\left.\frac{I_{Dnsat}}{I_{Dpsat}}\right|_{W_p=W_n}=\frac{\mu_n}{\mu_p}\]
