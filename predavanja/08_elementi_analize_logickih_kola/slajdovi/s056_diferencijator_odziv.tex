3. $u_o(\infty)$ — struja kondenzatora i otpornika iščezava.
\[u_0(\infty)=u_R(\infty)=Ri_R(\infty)=Ri_C(\infty)=0\]
\[\alert{u_o(t)=(U-U_o)e^{-\frac{t-t_0}{\tau}}},\qquad t\geq t_0\]
\begin{columns}[c,onlytextwidth]
\column{.37\textwidth}\centering\input{slike/tikz/s056_odziv_diferencijatora.tex}
\column{.59\textwidth}
\begin{align*}
i_R(t)&=i_C(t)=C\frac{du_C(t)}{dt}\\
u_C(t)&=u_i(t)-u_R(t)=u_i(t)-Ri_R(t)\\
i_R(t)&=C\frac{du_i(t)}{dt}-RC\frac{di_R(t)}{dt}\\
i_R(t)&\approx C\frac{du_i(t)}{dt}
\end{align*}
\end{columns}
\[u_i(t)=u_R(t)=Ri_R(t)\approx\alert{RC\frac{du_i(t)}{dt}}\]
