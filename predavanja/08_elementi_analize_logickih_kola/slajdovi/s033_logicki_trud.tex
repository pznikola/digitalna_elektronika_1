\begin{frame}[label=08-s033]{Logički trud - logical effort}
Savremena MOS kola: povezivanje dominantno utiče na \alert{dinamički režim} i brzinu rada.\par\medskip
\textbf{Logical effort — logički trud}\par\medskip
\begin{quote}\upshape
The logical effort of a logic gate is defined as the number of times worse it is at delivering output current than would be an inverter with identical input capacitance.\par\medskip
The logical effort of a logic gate is defined as the ratio of its input capacitance to that of an inverter that delivers equal output current.
\end{quote}
\end{frame}
