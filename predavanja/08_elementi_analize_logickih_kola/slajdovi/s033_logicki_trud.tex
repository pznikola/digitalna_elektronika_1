\begin{frame}[label=08-s033]{Logički trud - logical effort}
\gusttekst
Danas, za savremena logička kola realizovana u MOS tehnologiji dominantniji su elementi u povezivanju koji utiču na \alert{dinamički režim rada kola}, odnosno na njegovu brzinu rada. Pojam koji se pojavljuje jeste logical effort. Logički trud u najboljem prevodu. Evo nekih definicija u originalu, a na odgovarajućem mestu kada budemo analizirali ta logička kola ćemo diskutovati o tome.\par\bigskip
\begin{quote}\upshape
The logical effort of a logic gate is defined as the number of times worse it is at delivering output current than would be an inverter with identical input capacitance.\par\medskip
The logical effort of a logic gate is defined as the ratio of its input capacitance to that of an inverter that delivers equal output current.
\end{quote}
\end{frame}
