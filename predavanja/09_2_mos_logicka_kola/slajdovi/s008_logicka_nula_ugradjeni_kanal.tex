\begin{frame}[label=09_2-s008]{Napon logičke nule}

\[k_{n,L}(-V_{Tn,L})^2=k_{n,D}V_O\left(2(V_I-V_{Tn,D})-V_O\right)\]
Za mali $V_{OL}$ zanemarujemo njegov kvadrat.
\[k_{n,L}(-V_{Tn,L})^2=k_{n,D}V_O\left(2(V_{OH}-V_{Tn,D})\right)\]
\[V_{OL}\approx\frac12\frac{k_{n,L}}{k_{n,D}}\frac{(-V_{Tn,L})^2}{V_{DD}-V_{Tn,D}}\]
