\begin{frame}[label=10-s015]{Diskretni model i prekid karakteristike}
\setlength{\abovedisplayskip}{4pt}\setlength{\belowdisplayskip}{4pt}\setlength{\abovedisplayshortskip}{3pt}\setlength{\belowdisplayshortskip}{3pt}
Aktivni model:
\[V_O=V_{CC}-\beta_F\frac{R_C}{R_B}(V_I-0.6\,\mathrm{V})\]
Za $V_I=V_{\gamma T}=0.5\,\mathrm{V}$:
\[\begin{aligned}V_O&=V_{CC}-\beta_F\frac{R_C}{R_B}(V_{\gamma T}-0.6\,\mathrm{V})\\
&=V_{CC}-\beta_F\frac{R_C}{R_B}(0.5\,\mathrm{V}-0.6\,\mathrm{V})>V_{CC}.
\end{aligned}\]
\bigskip
Skok modela: $0.5\,\mathrm{V}\longrightarrow0.6\,\mathrm{V}$.\par
Smatramo da nema prekida pri $V_I=V_{\gamma T}$ i nastavljamo po izrazu
\[V_O=V_{CC}-\beta_F\frac{R_C}{R_B}(V_I-V_{BE}).\]
\end{frame}
