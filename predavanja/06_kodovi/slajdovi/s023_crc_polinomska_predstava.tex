\begin{frame}[label=06-s023]{CRC — Cyclic Redundancy Check}
\fontsize{10}{12}\selectfont\setlength{\abovedisplayskip}{3pt}\setlength{\belowdisplayskip}{3pt}
Predstava binarne vrednosti u obliku polinoma:
\[b_{n-1}\ldots b_1b_0\equiv b(X)=b_{n-1}X^{n-1}+b_{n-2}X^{n-2}+\cdots+b_1X+b_0\]
{\centering\input{tabele/s023_polinom.tex}\par}
\[1010100101\equiv X^9+X^7+X^5+X^2+1\]
Osnova CRC-a: generator polinom stepena $k$.
\[g(X)=g_kX^k+g_{k-1}X^{k-1}+g_{k-2}X^{k-2}+\cdots+g_1X+g_0\]
Dodaje se $k$ zaštitnih bita na osnovu ostatka deljenja:
\[\left(P\times X^k\right)\div g(X)\]
\centering\alert{Račun po modulu 2}
