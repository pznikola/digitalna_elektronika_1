\begin{frame}[label=06-s014]{Pokretni zarez — posebne vrednosti}
\setlength{\parskip}{5mm}
(a) If $1<E<254$, then $V=(-1)^S2^{E-127}(1.F)$ (normalized FP number)

(b) If $E=255$ and $F<>0$, then V=NAN (not a number)

(c) If $E=255$ and $F=0$, then $V=(-1)^S\infty$ (plus and minus infinity)

(d) If $E=0$ and $F<>0$, then $V=(-1)^S2^{-126}(0.F)$ (denormal, gradual underflow)

(e) If $E=0$ and $F=0$, then $V=(-1)^S0$ (positive and negative zero)
